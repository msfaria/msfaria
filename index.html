03 / THE WAY FORWARD

Small habits.
Better software.

Principles to grow with — in the classroom, in a new project, and beyond.

✳
  1. 01

    Understand before building.

    Ask questions, define the problem, and make a small plan before writing the first line.

  2. 02

    Keep it clear. Keep it kind.

    Write readable code, document the why, and design for the people who will use it.

  3. 03

    Test, reflect, repeat.

    Check assumptions, track changes with Git, and let feedback guide the next iteration.
